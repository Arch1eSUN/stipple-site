Stipple 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 6 日

Stipple（下称"本 App"）由个人开发者 YIXIAO SUN 开发和运营。本政策说明本 App 如何处理你的数据。

简要说明

  • 本 App 没有自己的服务器，也没有账号体系。开发者不收集你的任何个人数据，不做任何形式的跟踪。
  • 你连接的 AI 服务的登录凭据只保存在这台设备的钥匙串里，不上传 iCloud，也不发给开发者。
  • 额度数字、账号列表和偏好设置保存在你的设备上，并同步到你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库，开发者无法访问。
  • 本 App 不包含任何第三方分析、广告或跟踪 SDK。

本 App 是非官方集成

Stipple 与 OpenAI、Anthropic、GitHub、xAI、Google、OpenRouter、Moonshot（Kimi）、MiniMax、智谱（GLM）等服务商没有任何关联，也未获得其认可。本 App 在你本人登录或输入密钥后，从你的设备直接读取这些服务的官方客户端所使用的用量接口。这些接口不是公开承诺的开发者接口，服务商可能随时更改；改动后某些数字可能暂时读不到。

本 App 会处理哪些数据

  • 登录凭据：你登录或粘贴的访问令牌、API 密钥，以及部分平台网页登录后的会话 cookie。它们只存放在设备钥匙串中，仅用于向对应服务商请求你的用量数据，请求从你的设备直接发往服务商。凭据永不同步到 iCloud，每台设备需要各自登录。
  • 额度与用量数据：各窗口的已用比例、重置时间、额外额度、按天汇总的用量等，来自服务商对你账号的返回。
  • 账号列表与偏好：你关注了哪些账号、你给账号起的名字、提醒类型与阈值、显示方式等。

以上数据都不会发送给开发者或除对应服务商、Apple 以外的任何第三方。

iCloud 同步

当你的设备登录了 iCloud，本 App 会通过 CloudKit 把账号列表、偏好设置、额度快照和每日用量写入你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库，以便你的 iPhone、Mac 等设备显示同样的数据。其中额度与用量数值使用 CloudKit 端到端加密字段保存，Apple 与开发者都无法读取。这个数据库属于你的 Apple 账号，开发者没有访问权限。你可以在系统"设置 > [你的名字] > iCloud"中关闭本 App 的 iCloud，或管理、删除其中的数据。

Google 账号（Antigravity）

如果你用 Google 账号连接 Antigravity，本 App 会请求读取你的账号邮箱（用于区分不同账号）和访问 Antigravity 用量所需的 Google Cloud 权限。本 App 只用这些权限读取你的 Antigravity 额度并在设备上显示，不读取其它 Google 数据，不把 Google 数据用于广告，不出售或转让给任何人，也不交给开发者。本 App 对从 Google API 获得的信息的使用遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其中的 Limited Use 要求。

问答与提醒

App 内"问一问"的回答和提醒都在你的设备本地生成。提醒使用系统本地通知，不经过开发者的服务器。

移除账号与卸载

  • 在 App 内移除账号会删除本机保存的该账号凭据，并从你的 iCloud 数据中移除该账号；对支持撤销的服务商，本 App 会尽力请求撤销访问令牌。
  • iOS 卸载 App 时不会自动删除钥匙串里的条目。重新安装后首次启动时，本 App 会删除已不对应任何账号的旧凭据。如果你希望卸载前就清除，请先在 App 内移除账号。

第三方服务

  • 内购与订阅由 Apple 的 App Store 处理，开发者看不到你的支付信息。
  • 诊断：如果你在系统设置中选择"与 App 开发者共享"分析数据，Apple 会向开发者提供聚合的崩溃与性能报告。本 App 自身不收集诊断数据。

儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

政策更新

如本政策有实质性变更，我们会更新本页并注明新的生效日期。

Stipple Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Stipple ("the App") is developed and operated by an individual developer, YIXIAO SUN. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Summary

  • The App has no server and no account system of its own. The developer does not collect any personal data and does not track you.
  • Credentials for the AI services you connect are stored only in this device's Keychain. They are never uploaded to iCloud or sent to the developer.
  • Quota numbers, your account list, and your preferences are stored on your device and synced to your own private iCloud database, which the developer cannot access.
  • The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Stipple is an unofficial integration

Stipple is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Anthropic, GitHub, xAI, Google, OpenRouter, Moonshot (Kimi), MiniMax, Zhipu (GLM), or any other provider. After you sign in or enter a key yourself, the App reads usage directly from your device using the same endpoints those providers' own clients use. These endpoints are not published developer APIs and may change at any time, in which case some numbers may be temporarily unavailable.

What data the App handles

  • Credentials: access tokens and API keys you sign in with or paste, and, for some providers, the session cookie from an in-app web sign-in. They are stored only in the device Keychain and used only to request your usage from that provider, directly from your device. Credentials never sync to iCloud; each device signs in on its own.
  • Quota and usage data: used percentages, reset times, extra usage, daily usage totals, and similar values returned by the provider for your account.
  • Account list and preferences: which accounts you follow, names you give them, reminder types and thresholds, display options.

None of this data is sent to the developer or to any third party other than the relevant provider and Apple.

iCloud sync

When your device is signed in to iCloud, the App uses CloudKit to write your account list, preferences, quota snapshots, and daily usage to your own private iCloud database, so your iPhone, Mac, and other devices show the same data. Quota and usage values are stored in CloudKit's end-to-end encrypted fields, readable by neither Apple nor the developer. The database belongs to your Apple Account; the developer has no access to it. You can turn off iCloud for the App, or manage and delete its data, in Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

Google accounts (Antigravity)

If you connect Antigravity with a Google account, the App requests your account email (to tell accounts apart) and the Google Cloud permission needed to read Antigravity usage. The App uses these permissions only to read your Antigravity quota and show it on your device. It does not read other Google data, does not use Google data for advertising, does not sell or transfer it, and does not share it with the developer. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Answers and reminders

Answers in the App's "Ask" feature and all reminders are produced on your device. Reminders use local system notifications and never pass through a developer server.

Removing accounts and deleting the App

  • Removing an account in the App deletes that account's credentials from this device and removes the account from your iCloud data. For providers that support it, the App makes a best-effort request to revoke the access token.
  • iOS does not automatically delete Keychain items when you delete an app. On first launch after reinstalling, the App deletes old credentials that no longer belong to any account. To clear them before deleting the App, remove your accounts in the App first.

Third-party services

  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store. The developer never sees your payment details.
  • Diagnostics: if you choose "Share with App Developers" in system settings, Apple provides the developer with aggregated crash and performance reports. The App itself does not collect diagnostic data.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and note the new effective date.
