Stipple 技术支持

遇到问题、发现 bug 或有建议，请在 GitHub Issues 留言。请不要在留言里贴出任何密钥、令牌或包含账号信息的截图。

Stipple 是怎么读到我的额度的？

你在 App 里本人登录或粘贴密钥后，Stipple 从你的设备直接读取该服务官方客户端所使用的用量接口。这是非官方集成，与各服务商没有关联。每个平台能读到哪些信息（5 小时窗口、每周额度、额外额度、API 花费等）取决于该平台返回什么，以 App 内显示为准。

为什么某个数字读不到，或者平台不在列表里？

服务商可能随时更改这些接口。读不到时，Stipple 会显示"不可用"，不会估算或编造数字。没有可靠数据来源的平台，我们选择不支持。

我的密钥安全吗？

凭据只保存在这台设备的钥匙串里，不上传 iCloud，也不发给开发者；请求从你的设备直接发往对应服务商。详见隐私政策。

为什么换了设备要重新登录？

为了避免同一个登录令牌在多台设备间互相顶掉，凭据不做同步。额度数字会通过你的 iCloud 同步，所以另一台设备即使没登录，也能看到其它设备读到的额度。

怎么恢复购买？

打开付费页面，点"恢复购买"。需要用当初购买的同一个 Apple 账号登录。

怎么取消订阅？试用怎么计费？

订阅由 App Store 管理，请在 iPhone 的"设置 > [你的名字] > 订阅"中取消。月付、年付附带 7 天免费试用，试用期内取消不收费；试用结束后按所选档位自动续订，直到你取消。终身版为一次性购买。

卸载后凭据会被删除吗？

iOS 卸载 App 时不会自动删除钥匙串条目。重新安装后首次启动时，Stipple 会删除已不对应任何账号的旧凭据。想立即清除，请先在 App 内移除账号再卸载。

Stipple Support

For questions, bug reports, or suggestions, open an issue on GitHub. Please never post keys, tokens, or screenshots containing account details.

How does Stipple read my quota?

After you sign in or paste a key yourself, Stipple reads usage directly from your device using the endpoints the provider's own client uses. It is an unofficial integration, not affiliated with any provider. What you see for each platform (5-hour window, weekly quota, extra usage, API spend, and so on) depends on what that provider returns; the App shows exactly that.

Why is a number missing, or a platform not listed?

Providers can change these endpoints at any time. When a value can't be read, Stipple shows it as unavailable rather than estimating or making up a number. Platforms without a reliable data source are not supported.

Are my keys safe?

Credentials are stored only in this device's Keychain. They are never uploaded to iCloud or sent to the developer, and requests go straight from your device to the provider. See the Privacy Policy.

Why do I need to sign in again on another device?

Credentials are not synced, so the same sign-in token isn't refreshed by two devices at once. Quota numbers do sync through your iCloud, so a device that isn't signed in still shows what your other devices read.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the upgrade screen and tap "Restore Purchases", signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel? How does the trial work?

Subscriptions are managed by the App Store: go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. Monthly and yearly plans start with a 7-day free trial; cancel during the trial and you won't be charged. After the trial, the plan renews automatically until you cancel. Lifetime is a one-time purchase.

Are credentials deleted when I delete the App?

iOS does not delete Keychain items when an app is deleted. On first launch after reinstalling, Stipple deletes old credentials that no longer belong to any account. To clear them right away, remove your accounts in the App before deleting it.
